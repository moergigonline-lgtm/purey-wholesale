# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Lingerie and sleepwear shop owners in Libya buying wholesale from Purey (Tripoli, Al Rasheed Street). They browse mostly on phones, often arriving from TikTok or WhatsApp. The job: find models worth stocking, add them to an order basket in dozens (6 or 12 pieces), and send the order to the Purey team on WhatsApp (0919282214). Wholesale only; prices are confirmed by the team, never shown on the site.

## Product Purpose
A campaign-led Arabic (RTL) catalogue for Purey wholesale, with Lavermo, Purey's own limited-edition label, pushed as the premium choice. Success = shop owners trust the range at a glance, find pieces fast, and send complete orders. The AI assistant answers product, Lavermo, location and order questions without quoting prices.

## Brand Personality
Seductive, premium, confident. Reference: the Victoria's Secret / VS PINK split, with bold campaign photography and a clear two-brand switch. Wine (#8b1119), near-black and pure white carry the palette; Lavermo is the house label with its own script mark. Copy is Libyan Arabic, warm and direct.

## Anti-references
- Cream, beige, ivory or warm off-white surfaces anywhere (hard rule).
- Safe, "super basic" layouts: plain cards in rows, timid type, nothing distinctive.
- Cluttered marketplace look (price stickers, badges everywhere).
- Showing prices or amounts.

## Design Principles
1. Photography leads: campaign images do the selling; UI stays out of their way.
2. Two brands, one house: Purey and Lavermo are clearly separate choices, never mixed in the same pick.
3. Order in two taps: every product path ends in the basket or WhatsApp.
4. Phone first: decisions are checked at 360-390px before desktop.
5. Simple but premium: fewer elements, each one deliberate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast and 44px touch targets. Arabic RTL throughout, Latin only for brand names. Respect reduced motion.
